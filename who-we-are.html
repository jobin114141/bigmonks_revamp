2009 • THE SPARK

It all started in 2009, when a dream by our founder kicked off a series of events. It was the shaping of a lofty dream, as the seed of an idea was born.

THE KITCHEN HUB

The vision was so powerful and infectious that it attracted other like-minded souls as we shifted to a kitchen space in couple of years, turning an uninspiring but hallowed space into a vibrant hub for cooking ideas. Brewed by a different breed of people aspiring to build the future of retail, we soon moved out to a bigger facility.

TODAY • GLOBAL SCALE

Today, we are spread across global locations and are partners to some of the world’s leading enterprises and ambitious disruptors. Our relentless focus on zero-compromise engineering has enabled us to deliver over 250+ high-impact digital products worldwide.
